## 6. Client Context: Vocational IT Education & Student Acquisition Bottlenecks

**Soni Classes (Soni Computer Institute)** operates as a premier vocational training institute in Surat, Gujarat, specializing in computer literacy, office automation, financial accounting (Tally Prime, GST), graphic design, and basic software development.

In the vocational IT education sector, training institutes face three persistent operational challenges:
1. **Aggressive Local Competition**: Dozens of unaccredited computer centers compete heavily on price, creating noise in local search engines and making it difficult for quality institutes to stand out.
2. **Mobile-First Student & Parent Researchers**: High school graduates, college students, and working professionals search for skill-upgrade courses almost exclusively on smartphones. Slow-loading legacy sites result in high bounce rates before students ever view course modules or fee structures.
3. **Hyper-Local Radius Sensitivity**: Most vocational students prefer institutes within a 3 to 6 kilometer commuting distance. Broad regional marketing campaigns burn capital on students outside the institute's geographic reach.

When consulting **Vraj Vithalani**, Soni Classes required a modernized digital infrastructure to capture high-intent local student searches, showcase verified course certifications, and streamline inquiry-to-enrollment conversions.

### 1. Hyper-Local Google Business Profile & Map Pack Optimization
Vraj overhauled the institute's local search footprint, syncing primary categories (`Computer Training School`, `Accounting School`, `Educational Institution`) and publishing geotagged computer lab photography. By cultivating structured student review schema, Soni Classes achieved Top-3 map rankings for high-intent queries like *"best computer classes near me"*, *"Tally GST course in Surat"*, and *"graphic design institute in Adajan"*.

### 2. Sub-0.9s Mobile Course & Enrollment Portal
Vraj replaced an outdated legacy site with a custom, lightning-fast Next.js mobile web portal (< 0.9s load time). The portal featured sticky 1-tap WhatsApp consultation triggers, downloadable course syllabus PDFs, transparent batch timing schedules, and an instant demo class reservation form.

### 3. Structured Educational & Course Schema Architecture
By embedding `Course` and `EducationalOrganization` JSON-LD schema markup with explicit `provider`, `hasCourseInstance`, and `educationalCredentialAwarded` properties, Google search engines rich-indexed course modules directly into search results and AI answer engines.
