## 5. The B2B Export Challenge: Why Traditional PPC Fails Manufacturers

Indian manufacturers in hubs like **Surat, Ahmedabad, Rajkot, and Vadodara** often report burning ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 on Google Ads agency retainers only to receive irrelevant B2C retail queries, job seekers, or price-shopping casual callers.

### The B2B PPC Failure Cycle
1. **Broad Match Traps**: Agencies configure broad keywords like `power cable` or `synthetic graphite`, causing Google to show ads for `buy 1 meter cable for home` or `graphite pencil price`.
2. **Global Location Over-Extension**: Setting location targeting to "Worldwide" spends 80% of ad capital on low-conversion click networks or non-viable trade zones.
3. **Generic Corporate Sites**: Directing paid clicks to a slow (&gt; 3.5s) corporate WordPress homepage with generic "About Us" text instead of a technical product specification RFQ page.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (Pillar FAQs)

### Is ₹30,000 ($350 USD) enough for an initial B2B export Google Ads test?
Yes. A controlled ₹30,000 monthly ad spend (approx. ₹1,000/day or $12 USD/day) is sufficient to test overseas search demand when targeting tightly isolated, high-intent industrial keywords across 1–2 key export regions. With an average B2B CPC of ₹80–₹150, this yields 200–350 hyper-targeted clicks.

### How do you block retail and job-seeker search waste in B2B PPC?
Search waste is eliminated using a 3-layer negative keyword strategy: B2C & Retail Terms, Employment/Career Terms, and Informational/DIY Terms, ensuring 100% commercial procurement intent.

### Should B2B exporters target global broad locations or specific countries?
Never target "All Countries" or global broad radiuses in B2B Google Ads. Tier your geographic target into 2–3 specific high-margin buyer regions based on shipping logistics and tariff advantages.

### What essential elements must a B2B RFQ landing page contain?
Technical specifications upfront, downloadable PDF technical datasheets, 4-field RFQ form (Name, Work Email, Quantity, Destination Port), direct WhatsApp Exporters Desk CTA, and sub-1.2s mobile page load speed.
