### Section 2: Data Collection Parameters & Purpose Limitation
We collect personal information solely when voluntarily provided by users through website lead capture forms, direct email communications, or scheduled consultation requests.

#### Collected Data Points:
- **Full Name**: Used to personalize project communications and verification.
- **Email Address**: Used for official proposals, audit reports, and consultation scheduling.
- **Phone Number / WhatsApp Contact**: Used for direct project calls, status updates, and authorized project communication.
- **Company / Website URL**: Used to perform pre-consultation technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, and Google Ads audits.
- **Budget Brackets & Project Details**: Used to evaluate project scope and service compatibility.

#### Purpose Limitation & Zero-Spam Guarantee:
- Personal data is processed **exclusively** to respond to incoming service inquiries, deliver requested marketing/development audits, and execute active client agreements.
- We **never** sell, rent, trade, or lease personal lead data to third-party brokers, advertising networks, or lead generators.
- Unsolicited marketing emails and automated newsletter blasts are strictly prohibited.

---

### Section 3: Analytics, Tracking & Dynamic Scripts
To ensure high-performance web experiences and monitor campaign conversions, this platform utilizes first-party analytics and tracking technologies:
- **Google Tag Manager (GTM) & Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**: Used to measure aggregate site interactions, page performance, and campaign attribution without storing raw personal identifiers.
- **Meta Pixel & Conversions API (CAPI)**: Used to measure ad conversion efficiency using cryptographic SHA-256 hashing for user data privacy.
- **Microsoft Clarity**: Used to record session replays and heatmaps to identify user UX friction points without capturing form keystrokes or sensitive input fields.
- **Dynamic Script Management**: Third-party scripts are dynamically injected via Next.js `Script` components (`strategy="afterInteractive"`) managed securely through the internal `/iamadmin/settings/` dashboard.

---

### Section 4: Data Retention & Security Standards
- **Retention Schedule**: Unconverted lead submission records are retained in an encrypted MongoDB Atlas database for a maximum of **90 days**, after which they are permanently purged. Active client data is retained for the duration of the engagement plus statutory tax record requirements.
- **Database Security**: All lead data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS and encrypted at rest within MongoDB Atlas clusters utilizing AES-256 standards.
- **Spam Prevention (Honeypot Trap)**: Lead forms utilize an invisible honeypot field (`website_url`). Bot submissions triggering this trap are silently rejected with a fake `200 OK` response without saving data to our database.

---

### Section 5: User Rights under India’s DPDP Act 2023
In compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act)**, users possess the following explicit rights regarding their personal data:
1. **Right to Access & Summary**: The right to request a digital copy of all personal data held under your identity.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure**: The right to request immediate rectification of inaccurate data or permanent deletion of your lead record ("Right to be Forgotten").
3. **Right to Grievance Redressal**: The right to submit formal data privacy inquiries directly to the Data Protection Grievance Officer.

To exercise any of these rights, email **`vrajvithalani02@gmail.com`** with the subject line *"DPDP Data Erasure Request"*. Erasure requests are processed within 48 business hours.
