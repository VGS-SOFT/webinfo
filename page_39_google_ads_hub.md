## 3. Foundation Before You Launch Anything

Before spending a single rupee on Google Ads, your technical foundation must be locked down. Launching ads without proper account structure or conversion tracking is burning capital.

### Single-Campaign Account Architecture
For budgets under ₹50,000/month, spreading capital across 5 different campaigns splits your daily impression share and prevents algorithm learning. 

We recommend launching a **Single-Campaign Search Architecture**:
* **One Main Search Campaign**: Geofenced to your primary operating territory.
* **3 to 5 Tightly Themed Ad Groups**: Grouped strictly by specific service lines or product families.
* **5 to 10 Exact and Phrase Keywords per Ad Group**: Zero unvetted broad match keywords at launch.

### The Minimum Pre-Launch Tracking Stack
Never launch a campaign relying solely on default pageview metrics. Before setting campaigns live, ensure your 4-piece tracking stack is active and tested:
1. **Google Tag Manager (GTM)**: Container deployed in `<head>` for zero-latency event triggering.
2. **GA4 Custom Event Stream**: Tracking primary conversion events (`generate_lead`, `click_to_call`, `whatsapp_inquiry`).
3. **Google Ads Conversion Tags**: Primary conversion actions linked directly to GTM triggers with enhanced conversions enabled.
4. **Google Search Console**: Verified and linked to monitor organic-versus-paid keyword cannibalization.

## 4. Search Campaigns for Local Service Businesses

Local service businesses—whether healthcare clinics, travel agencies, coaching centers, or repair specialists—live and die by local intent search volume.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 LOCAL SERVICE SEARCH CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOK                          │
├───────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Match-Type Strategy           │ • Phase 1: Exact + Phrase Match starting mix    │
│                               │ • Phase 2: Broad Match + tCPA (only post-30 leads)│
├───────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Negative Keyword Scrubbing    │ • Pre-launch list: free, jobs, salary, pdf, gov │
│                               │ • Weekly search-terms report audit              │
├───────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Credibility Ad Copy           │ • Highlight 100% direct specialist, verified reviews│
│                               │ • Transparent price range & instant response    │
└───────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Match-Type Control**: Start with a strict 70% Exact Match / 30% Phrase Match ratio. Avoid Broad Match until your account records at least 30 verified conversions per month.
* **Negative Keyword Defense**: Pre-load your campaign with a 500+ master negative list filtering out job seekers (`vacancy`, `salary`, `career`), free seekers (`free`, `pdf`, `course material`), and government portals (`irctc`, `gov.in`, `tender`).
* **Anti-Race-to-Bottom Ad Copy**: When competitors price-dump, do not compete on price. Frame your headlines around speed, direct practitioner execution, verified case outcomes, and instant response guarantees.

## 5. Performance Max Without a Full Creative Team

Performance Max (PMax) can be a powerful channel, but Google's automated asset generation often produces low-quality visuals and irrelevant placements if left unsupervised.

### The Lean SMB PMax Setup
If you lack a dedicated creative agency, follow these strict guardrails:
* **Service-Line Asset Group Mapping**: Create separate asset groups for distinct service lines rather than dumping all headlines into one bucket.
* **Manual Asset Uploads**: Upload high-resolution, real photos of your facility, products, or practitioner team. Never rely on auto-generated stock slides.
* **Exclude Low-Quality Display Placements**: Use campaign-level placement exclusions to block mobile game apps and low-intent display networks that waste ad clicks.
* **Feed-Driven PMax for E-Commerce**: For D2C brands, ensure your Google Merchant Center product feed is fully sanitized with GTIN, brand, and custom label attributes before launching PMax.

Marketers blame the developer when landing pages load slowly; developers blame the marketer when traffic doesn't convert. The real growth leverage sits at the intersection of **running the campaign AND engineering the page**.

* **Dual Conversion Triggers**: Provide both a sticky 1-tap WhatsApp consultation button for mobile users and a 3-field form for desktop visitors.

## 7. Diagnosing Underperforming Campaigns

When a campaign fails to produce inquiries, agency account managers often tell clients to *"increase daily budget."* In 90% of cases, the problem is structural.

Run this **30-Minute Diagnostic Sequence**:

```
                              [Campaign Underperforming]
                                          │
                  ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                  ▼                                               ▼
         [Low CTR (< 3.0%)]                             [High CTR (> 5.0%)]
                  │                                               │
     ┌────────────┴────────────┐                     ┌────────────┴────────────┐
     ▼                         ▼                     ▼                         ▼
[Irrelevant Query]     [Weak Copy / Extensions] [No Conversions]     [Slow Landing Page]
  Scrub Negatives        Rewrite Headlines       Check Tracking          Optimize Speed
```

1. **Check CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: If CTR is below 3.0% on Search, your ad copy lacks message match or your match types are pulling in irrelevant queries.
2. **Inspect Search-Terms Report**: Review actual user search terms. If more than 20% of clicks are non-commercial, add negative keywords immediately.
3. **Verify Landing Page Conversion Rate**: If CTR is high (> 5.0%) but conversion rate is under 2.0%, test page load speed, inspect form functionality, or rewrite the primary headline.

## 8. Budget Realities & Test Protocols for Indian SMBs

### The ₹15,000/Month Test Protocol
You do not need ₹1,00,000 to test Google Ads. A structured 30-day test at **₹500/day (₹15,000/month)** yields actionable data:
* **Week 1 (Setup & Launch)**: Single campaign, 2 ad groups, 10 exact/phrase keywords, manual CPC bidding.
* **Week 2 (Negative Scrubbing)**: Daily search-term inspection, adding 15–20 negative keywords daily.
* **Week 3 (Ad Copy Optimization)**: Pause low-performing ad variations; double down on headlines with the highest CTR.
* **Week 4 (Data Read & Scale Decision)**: Calculate Cost Per Lead (CPL). If CPL is within target margins, increase budget by 20% increments.

## 13. 10 Pillar-Level Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the minimum monthly ad spend required for Google Ads in India?
For local service businesses in India, a starting test budget of ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 per month is sufficient to gather statistical CPC signal and generate initial leads when concentrated into a single, tightly themed Search campaign.

### Q2: Why do my Google Ads get clicks but no phone calls or lead form submissions?
In 80% of audited accounts, clicks without conversions stem from three issues: broad match keyword over-reach attracting informational query waste, slow mobile page load speed (> 2.5s), or ad copy mismatching the landing page headline offer.

### Q3: Should a small business use Performance Max or Search campaigns first?
Small businesses with limited ad budgets and no dedicated creative team should launch with Search campaigns first. Performance Max requires rich creative assets and existing conversion volume (30+ conversions/month) to train Smart Bidding algorithms effectively.

### Q4: How long does it take for Google Ads to generate reliable leads?
Search campaigns with proper GTM tracking and exact/phrase keyword targeting generate qualified leads within 3 to 7 days of launch. The first 14 days serve as a learning period to refine negative keywords and bid pacing.

### Q5: How does landing page speed affect Google Ads CPC and Quality Score?
Google Ads calculates Quality Score partially based on Landing Page Experience. Pages loading in under 1.2s achieve higher Quality Scores (8/10 to 10/10), which directly reduces Cost Per Click (CPC) by 15% to 30% compared to slow competitors.

### Q6: What conversion tracking tools should be set up before launching Google Ads?
The minimum pre-launch tracking stack consists of Google Tag Manager (GTM), GA4 event triggers, Google Ads Conversion Tags (with primary lead form and click-to-call actions), and Google Search Console integration.

### Q7: When is it safe to switch from Manual CPC to Smart Bidding (tCPA)?
Smart Bidding (Target CPA or Maximize Conversions) is safe once an account records at least 30 verified conversions within a rolling 30-day window. Below this threshold, manual or enhanced CPC prevents algorithmic budget waste.

### Q8: Can B2B industrial exporters get overseas buyers through Google Ads?
Yes, by geo-targeting specific buyer countries (US, UAE, Europe), using strict B2B negative keywords ('retail', 'scrap rate', 'jobs'), and building custom RFQ landing pages, Indian exporters routinely capture high-intent international procurement leads.

### Q9: What is the difference between a Google Ads freelancer and an agency?
Agencies often assign accounts to junior account managers and charge recurring percentage-of-spend markups. An independent specialist like Vraj Vithalani personally manages campaign mechanics, landing page code, and tracking setups with direct communication.
