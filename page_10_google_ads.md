## 5. The 12-Point Forensic Google Ads Audit & Engineering Framework

To eliminate ad spend waste and build scalable PPC campaigns, Vraj Vithalani applies a strict 12-point engineering framework across every client account:

1. **Search Term Isolation & Match Type Sculpting**:
   Filtering out broad-match bleed by isolating Exact Match and Intent-Driven Phrase Match queries into tight thematic clusters.
2. **Negative Keyword Barrier Sculpting**:
   Deploying universal and campaign-level negative lists (e.g., "free", "jobs", "wholesale PDF", "meaning", "course") to block non-commercial clicks instantly.
3. **Quality Score & Relevancy Alignment**:
   Matching Ad Copy headlines directly to Landing Page H1s and user search intent, driving Quality Scores from 4/10 to 8-10/10 to reduce Cost-Per-Click (CPC) by 15–30%.
4. **Conversion Tracking Integrity Audit**:
   Verifying GA4 primary conversion events, Google Tag Manager (GTM) triggers, and Server-Side Meta/Google CAPI to eliminate double-counting or unrecorded leads.
5. **Enhanced Conversions & Offline Data Imports**:
   Injecting hashed user lead data (`sha256_email`, `sha256_phone`) back into Google Ads to feed Smart Bidding algorithms with true closed-won CRM values.
6. **Smart Bidding vs. Manual CPC Strategy Selection**:
   Utilizing Manual CPC with Enhanced CPC during campaign launch phases, transitioning to Target CPA or Target ROAS only after achieving 30+ verified conversions per month.
7. **Ad Copy & Responsive Search Ad (RSA) Engineering**:
   Crafting 15 distinct, pin-optimized RSA headlines and 4 descriptions leveraging dynamic keyword insertion (DKI) and explicit value propositions.
8. **Extension & Asset Optimization**:
   Deploying Sitelink extensions, Callout assets, Structured Snippets, Image extensions, and Call assets to maximize Search Result SERP real estate.
9. **Geographic & Demographics Radius Sculpting**:
   Excluding non-converting zip codes, setting bid adjustments for high-income demographics, and setting precise hyper-local geo-fencing (e.g., Surat GIDC industrial zones).
10. **Device & Dayparting Schedule Tuning**:
    Adjusting bidding curves based on historical desktop vs. mobile conversion rates and business hour phone response capabilities.
11. **Landing Page Speed & Friction Removal**:
    Ensuring destination pages achieve sub-1.2s Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) speeds and zero form friction, directly boosting conversion rate (CR).
12. **Competitor Auction Insights & Bidding Defense**:
    Monitoring Impression Share (Search IS lost to budget vs. lost to rank) to strategically out-position competitors on high-value commercial keywords.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (Technical FAQ Accordion)

### FAQ 1: What is the recommended minimum monthly ad spend for Google Ads in India?
> For B2B lead generation or local services, a starting ad budget of **₹25,000 to ₹50,000 per month** is recommended. This provides sufficient click volume to test keywords, isolate negative search terms, and gather statistical data for conversion optimization within 30 days.

### FAQ 2: Do I retain ownership of my Google Ads account and campaign data?
> Yes, 100%. All campaigns are built directly inside **your own Google Ads account**. You grant admin or standard access to Vraj Vithalani. If you ever pause services, all campaign structures, historical data, and negative keyword lists remain entirely your property.

### FAQ 3: How long does it take to launch a new Google Ads campaign?
> New account setups and campaign re-architectures take **3 to 5 business days**. This window includes comprehensive keyword research, search term isolation, RSA ad copy drafting, GTM conversion tag installation, and client review before going live.

### FAQ 4: How do you prevent competitors or spambots from clicking my ads?
> We employ multi-layered click fraud prevention: implementing IP exclusion lists, geo-fencing strict location boundaries, blocking non-commercial search intent via negative sculpting, and setting up client-side invisible honeypot form fields to eliminate bot submissions.

### FAQ 5: What is the pricing structure for Google Ads management?
> Google Ads services operate on a transparent **flat monthly retainer** based on account complexity and scope—not a percentage of ad spend. This ensures complete alignment: Vraj's goal is to maximize your ROI and lower your CPL, not force you to spend more on Google to increase agency fees.

### FAQ 6: How do you track telephone call conversions from Google Ads?
> Call tracking is implemented using Google Call Extensions and GTM click triggers on mobile phone links. Call duration thresholds (e.g., calls lasting over 60 seconds) are logged in GA4 as primary conversions to ensure ad algorithms optimize for genuine customer conversations.
