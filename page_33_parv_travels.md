## 6. Client Context: Regional Travel & Fleet Rental Bottlenecks

**Parv Travels** is a regional travel agency and commercial fleet rental operator providing customized tour packages, luxury bus charters, and outstation car rentals.

In the regional travel and hospitality sector, PPC campaigns frequently suffer from severe budget leaks:
1. **Informational & Government Search Inflation**: Unoptimized travel campaigns waste up to 60% of budget on queries like *"train schedule"*, *"IRCTC ticket booking"*, *"GSRTC bus timing"*, or *"free holiday itinerary"*.
2. **Mobile Friction during Seasonal Spikes**: Travelers searching for weekend getaways, wedding bus rentals, or corporate tours search heavily on mobile devices. A slow website or complex 8-field form causes potential bookers to drop off immediately and call a competitor.
3. **Lack of High-Intent Route Isolation**: Generic ads directed to an unoptimized homepage fail to match specific traveler needs (e.g., someone searching *"35 seater AC bus rental in Surat"* needs immediate vehicle photos, pricing tiers, and capacity specs, not generic travel text).

When consulting **Vraj Vithalani**, Parv Travels needed a surgical Google Search Ads audit and a rapid mobile conversion funnel to capture high-ticket travel bookings.

## 7. The Growth Strategy: Search Isolation & Frictionless Booking UX

To eliminate ad waste and maximize travel inquiry conversions, Vraj deployed a 3-stage PPC and CRO engine:

### 1. Forensic Negative Keyword Sculpting
Vraj audited search query reports and added 600+ negative keywords blocking non-commercial traffic, including government transport terms (*IRCTC, GSRTC, Railway, Government Bus*), job searches (*driver vacancy, travel job*), and free information terms (*map, distance, weather forecast*).

### 2. Fleet & Package SADDs (Single Impression Ad Groups)
Ad campaigns were re-architected into tightly themed ad groups matching exact user intent—separating *Corporate Bus Rentals*, *Family Outstation Cabs*, and *Domestic Tour Packages*. Ad copy dynamically reflected vehicle seating capacity and transparent per-kilometer rates.

### 3. Sub-0.9s Mobile Booking Interface
Vraj replaced the slow legacy site with a high-speed Next.js mobile booking interface. The lander featured sticky 1-tap WhatsApp consultation buttons, vehicle photo galleries, transparent pricing structures, and an instant 3-step quote request form.
