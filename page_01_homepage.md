## 10. Section 8: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Accordion)

### FAQ 1: Why hire a single growth engineer instead of a digital marketing agency?
**Answer**: Agencies bill high monthly retainers to pay for account managers, sales reps, and office overhead, while assigning your actual work to junior interns. When you work with Vraj Vithalani, 100% of your budget goes directly into senior-level engineering execution. You communicate directly with the engineer coding your site and managing your ad budgets, resulting in faster iteration, better transparency, and superior performance.

### FAQ 2: What guarantee do you offer for Core Web Vitals and site speed?
**Answer**: Every Next.js 15 site I develop is backed by a strict performance SLA: 100/100 Google Lighthouse speed scores, Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) under 1.2 seconds, and zero Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS = 0.00). If a site fails to meet these benchmarks upon launch, I resolve it at zero extra charge.

### FAQ 3: How do you prevent spambots from flooding contact forms?
**Answer**: I employ a client-side invisible honeypot trap field (`website_url`) hidden via absolute CSS positioning (`-top-[9999px]`). Automated spambots fill this field automatically, triggering an immediate server-side silent rejection that returns a fake `200 OK` response without storing garbage data in MongoDB or cluttering your inbox.

### FAQ 4: How fast can a Google Ads campaign start generating qualified leads?
**Answer**: Search campaigns typically go live within 48 to 72 hours following initial audit and tracking setup. Inbound calls and leads usually begin within the first 24 to 48 hours of campaign launch, followed by continuous negative keyword and bid optimization during the first 14 days.

### FAQ 5: What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and why is it required in 2026?
**Answer**: GEO is the practice of optimizing digital content so that generative AI search engines (*ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude*) easily parse, cite, and recommend your brand. By embedding concise 60–80 word direct answer summaries and Schema.org `@graph` metadata into your pages, your business secures prime visibility in AI-generated answers.

### FAQ 6: What budget is required to start working with Vraj Vithalani?
**Answer**: While campaign requirements vary by industry and location, most search ad clients start with a minimum monthly ad spend of ₹25,000 to ₹50,000, plus a flat management fee. Fixed web development and technical SEO projects are quoted transparently based on route complexity.
